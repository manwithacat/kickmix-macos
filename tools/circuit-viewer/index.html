Inside invsqrt

The circuit must multiply each basis state |x⟩ by the phase e2πi/√x for every x from 1 to 3,133,452, and hand x back untouched with every scratch qubit at zero. Holding a 1 is only a lower bound on being in use: a qubit carrying data can read 0. Q = 63 because all 41 scratch qubits are needed at some point and their lifetimes overlap in a staggered way (the darker background in the raster), so saving a qubit means shortening or re-packing those lifetimes, not just lowering the peak.

What would it cost to build?

For fun, and very roughly. This circuit is tiny by quantum-algorithm standards, but running it on real hardware needs error correction, and that multiplies everything. Move the sliders; every number below is an assumption, not a quote.

Physical qubits per logical qubit ≈ 2d². Higher d means fewer errors and more qubits.
Extra space for moving logical qubits around (lattice surgery).
Each one makes the resource states Toffoli gates consume, one Toffoli's worth per d rounds; about 3,000 physical qubits apiece assumed. More factories, faster shots.
A rough 2026 order of magnitude for a superconducting system with cryogenics and control electronics, $10k to $200k (log scale).
One surface-code round: about 1 µs for superconducting qubits, about 1 ms for neutral atoms. Shot time ≈ Toffolis ÷ factories × d rounds.

Circuit: mrmeows' 63-qubit hybrid invsqrt build (public dataset qcc-1375-open-circuits, Apache-2.0; .kmb SHA-256 0766a111…07d67d6), on the public line of busyaprime, Phuc Thai, Dmitrii Gluzdov and marsyas. Simulator and verifier: Google Quantum AI's kickmix.

How this page computes: a bit-level tracer runs the circuit in your browser for one basis state. Each measurement draws an outcome from a seeded generator, and the phase is kept exactly in units of 2⁻¹²⁸ turn. On mrmeows' circuit the tracer matched kickmix exactly (all qubits, all classical bits and the phase, on 2,299 inputs with outcome-matched replay checks). The precision curve and the occupancy maximum were computed offline with kickmix and the same tracer.

Your own circuit: a loaded .kmx file stays in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. The page is a viewer, not a verifier: the official verifier is the referee. Convert a .kmb with kickmix.Circuit.from_file("c.kmb").to_file("c.kmx", format="kmx"). Circuits much larger than invsqrt (about 50,000 ops) may be slow in a browser.

Stage names: stages tagged documented follow mrmeows' published README and proofs; those tagged inferred are read from the op stream, with boundaries good to about ±100 ops. Built with AI assistance (Claude) for manwithacat.

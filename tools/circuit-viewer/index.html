Inside invsqrt

The circuit must multiply each basis state |x⟩ by the phase e2πi/√x for every x from 1 to 3,133,452, and hand x back untouched with every scratch qubit at zero. Accumulated phase

The dial shows the phase as an angle: coloured hand = phase so far (at the playhead when it is paused), black tick = target 1/√x.

An HMR that reads 1 on a qubit holding 1 adds half a turn, and a later Z or CZ conditioned on that reading pays it back, so mid-circuit phases (the lookup's especially) flash as x changes. This toggle replays with every HMR outcome forced to 0: no kickback, no fix-ups, and the same final phase.

What would it cost to build?

For fun, and very roughly. This circuit is tiny by quantum-algorithm standards, but running it on real hardware needs error correction, and that multiplies everything. Move the sliders; every number below is an assumption, not a quote.

Physical qubits per logical qubit ≈ 2d². Higher d means fewer errors and more qubits.
Extra space for moving logical qubits around (lattice surgery).
Each one makes the resource states Toffoli gates consume, one Toffoli's worth per d rounds; about 3,000 physical qubits apiece assumed. More factories, faster shots.
A rough 2026 order of magnitude for a superconducting system with cryogenics and control electronics, $10k to $200k (log scale).
One surface-code round: about 1 µs for superconducting qubits, about 1 ms for neutral atoms. Shot time ≈ Toffolis ÷ factories × d rounds.

How this page computes: a bit-level tracer runs the circuit in your browser for one basis state. Each measurement draws an outcome from a seeded generator, and the phase is kept exactly in units of 2⁻¹²⁸ turn. On all three demo circuits the tracer matched kickmix exactly (all qubits, all classical bits and the phase, on 2,299 inputs each, including x = 1, 161 and the maximum, with outcome-matched replays). The precision curve and the occupancy maximum were computed offline with kickmix and the same tracer.

Your own circuit: a loaded .kmx file stays in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. The page is a viewer, not a verifier: the official verifier is the referee. Convert a .kmb with kickmix.Circuit.from_file("c.kmb").to_file("c.kmx", format="kmx"). Circuits much larger than invsqrt (about 50,000 ops) may be slow in a browser.

Stage names: stages tagged documented follow mrmeows' published README and proofs (and, for the re-synthesised lookup and window S1, busyaprime's notebook); those tagged inferred are read from the op stream, with boundaries good to about ±100 ops. On busyaprime's circuit the stage boundaries were carried over from mrmeows' file by an op-by-op diff: outside the two re-synthesised regions the two op streams are identical. On the 62-qubit circuit they are busyaprime's 17 stages carried over again: where the two op streams run the same gate sequence on relabelled wires (most of the decoder, arithmetic, lookup and seed-retirement stages) a boundary moves with its block, and elsewhere it sits at the matching op pattern, checked against the live-wire values.
